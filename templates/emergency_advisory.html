DO NOT WAIT FOR A ROUTINE APPOINTMENT

If you or someone in your care is experiencing symptoms of a potential life-threatening emergency, call local emergency services (911 / 112) immediately or proceed directly to our Emergency Department.

  • Crushing chest pain, left arm pain, or severe heart palpitations
  • Sudden weakness, facial droop, numbness, or difficulty speaking (Stroke signs)
  • Severe shortness of breath, choking, or inability to breathe
  • Major traumatic injury, uncontrolled bleeding, or suspected bone fractures
  • Loss of consciousness, seizures, or acute severe allergic reactions (Anaphylaxis)

⚖️ Clinical Triage & Urgency Assessment Protocol

To ensure patient safety, patients do not assign themselves a clinical priority. Qualified emergency medical personnel perform a physiological clinical assessment upon your arrival and assign an objective triage category:

Critical (Red)

Immediate resuscitation, airway or cardiovascular threat.

Urgent (Orange)

Severe pain, acute distress, potential rapid deterioration.

Less Urgent (Green)

Stable emergency presentations and standard clinical care.
